Claude Battery

Your Claude usage at a glance. A lightweight macOS menu bar app that turns confusing token counts into a simple battery.

Download Mac (v1.50) Other ways to install

Lives quietly in your menu bar.
No analytics. No tracking. No data collection.

Instantly understandable

Full means you're good. Half means pace yourself. Low means take a break. No token math required.

Session and weekly tracking

Two batteries at a glance -- your 5-hour session limit and your 7-day weekly quota, with reset countdown timers.

Low usage alerts

Get a notification when your weekly quota drops below a threshold you set. Never get caught off guard again.

Multiple accounts

Sign into up to 5 Claude accounts -- work, personal, side projects. Switch between them instantly.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Left-click for details. Right-click for settings. That's it.
